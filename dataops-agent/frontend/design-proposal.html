02 — Derived scales
Light & dark token sets

Text/ink stays cool (derived from Midnight's blue hue) in both themes; surfaces stay warm (derived from Pearl Perfect's cream undertone) in light mode. Ocean is the one accent used for links, active states, and focus rings in both themes — lighter tints in dark mode for contrast against Noir. Semantic colors (success/warning/danger) keep their exact hues; only the background/border tints change per theme.
